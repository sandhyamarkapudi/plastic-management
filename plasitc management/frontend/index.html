Source Sorting System

The 4-Bin Household System

Every home can keep village surroundings clean by sorting everyday rubbish into these four easy groups:

Green Bin

Wet & Organic Waste

Vegetable peels, fruit waste, left-over food, tea grounds, garden leaves. Perfect for natural village compost!

Blue Bin

Dry Clean Packaging

Cardboard cartons, old newspapers, paper bags, metal cans, glass jars. Keep clean and free from food oils.

Yellow Bin

Plastic Items & Bottles

Water bottles, milk packets, oil containers, snack packaging. Empty, rinse with a splash of water, and store dry.

Red Bin

Sanitary & Hazardous

Batteries, medical blister packs, sanitary items, pesticide pouches. Keep wrapped securely for designated disposal.
