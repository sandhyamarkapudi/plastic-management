Everyday Habits

Good Habits Keep Villages Clean & Healthy

A quick, clear guide on actions that heal our village environment versus common practices that inadvertently poison our air, water, and cattle.

Approved Practices

DO'S (Do Daily)

  • Carry a reusable cloth or jute bag whenever heading to the grocery store or weekly village market (shandy/haat).
  • Separate plastic waste from wet food at home so organic scraps can turn into compost and plastic can be recycled.
  • Rinse empty milk packets and oil bottles with a quick splash of water to keep insects away and prevent foul odor.
  • Reuse sturdy food-safe containers (curd tubs, pickle jars) for organizing seeds, pulses, or garden planters.
  • Support your Gram Panchayat sanitation team by handing over dry recyclables separately every collection day.
  • Opt for traditional alternatives like banana leaves, areca palm plates, and steel tumblers during village festivals and weddings.
Strictly Prohibited

DON'TS (Avoid Completely)

  • Never throw food scraps inside tied plastic bags. Stray cattle eat the plastic along with the food, leading to painful blockages and death.
  • Never burn plastic waste or dry leaves mixed with plastic. The toxic black smoke releases cancer-causing furans and damages children's lungs.
  • Never dump bottles or polythene in village drains or canals. Choked drains cause rainwater flooding and mosquito infestations.
  • Never mix oily curry, sambar, or meat leftovers with dry plastic. Food contamination ruins the recyclability of an entire batch.
  • Never pack steaming hot tea or curries in thin plastic pouches. High heat leaches endocrine-disrupting chemicals directly into food.
  • Never leave plastic pesticide bottles in agricultural fields. Return them to dealers or dispose of them strictly in hazardous collection streams.
Scenario Guides

Practical Advice for Village Events

At the Weekly Bazaar (Shandy)

Keep 2 canvas bags in your two-wheeler or bicycle. Ask vendors to weigh vegetables loose together in one bag instead of using 10 flimsy thin pouches.

Weddings & Village Celebrations

Ban single-use plastic water bottles. Provide reusable stainless steel glasses and large filtered water dispensers. Serve food on compostable areca leaves.

On Farmland & Crop Fields

Inspect manure heaps before applying to soil. Remove torn mulch films and fertiliser sacks. Never set field bunds on fire if plastic is present.
