Resin Identification System

Decoding Plastic Types 1 to 7

Look at the bottom of any container for the triangular chasing arrows symbol with a number inside. Here is what every code means for your health and village recycling.

1 PETE
High Value
Polyethylene Terephthalate

Water & Beverage Bottles

Clear, rigid, and lightweight. Most commonly collected and reprocessed by scrap recyclers.

Recyclability: 95% (Widely Recycled)
Everyday Examples:
Mineral Water Cold Drinks Cooking Oil Bottles

Action: Empty completely, crush flat to save space, store dry, and give to local collection.

2 HDPE
Food Safe
High-Density Polyethylene

Milk, Shampoo & Detergents

Thick, opaque, sturdy plastic with very low chemical leaching risk. Highly desirable for recycling.

Recyclability: 90% (Widely Recycled)
Everyday Examples:
Milk Packets/Cans Shampoo Bottles Bucket & Crates

Action: Quick rinse with water to remove residue, let dry, keep separate for scrap pickup.

3 PVC
Never Burn
Polyvinyl Chloride

Pipes, Flooring & Blister Packs

Contains chlorine plasticizers. Emits highly toxic dioxin smoke if burned. Difficult to recycle mechanically.

Recyclability: 15% (Hard to Recycle)
Everyday Examples:
Plumbing Pipes Electrical Conduits Plastic Sheeting

Critical: NEVER burn. Hand off scrap pipe cuttings strictly to hardware scrap dealers.

4 LDPE
Clean Film Only
Low-Density Polyethylene

Carry Bags & Cling Film

Soft, flexible, and stretchable. When contaminated with food or dirt, it easily clogs processing machinery.

Recyclability: 55% (Moderate)
Everyday Examples:
Grocery Bags Bread Bags Bubble Wrap

Action: Minimize single-use bags. Stuff clean dry bags inside one bag for bundle collection.

5 PP
Heat Resistant
Polypropylene

Food Tubs, Caps & Straws

High melting point, durable, and safe for warm food containers. Increasingly valued by recyclers.

Recyclability: 80% (High Potential)
Everyday Examples:
Yogurt/Curd Tubs Bottle Caps Medicine Bottles

Action: Excellent for home reuse (storing seeds, spices, sewing kits). When worn, recycle clean.

6 PS
Avoid Single Use
Polystyrene (Styrofoam)

Foam Cups & Disposable Plates

Lightweight and brittle foam. Breaks down into billions of micro-pellets that blow into ponds and fields.

Recyclability: 10% (Rarely Recycled)
Everyday Examples:
Tea/Coffee Foam Cups Wedding Feast Plates Appliance Packaging

Best Practice: Replace with traditional banana leaves, areca nut palm plates, or steel glasses.

7 OTHER
Multi-Layer
Multi-Layered Plastics (MLP)

Chips Bags, Biscuits & Sachets

Contains laminated sandwiches of plastic film and aluminum foil. Cannot be recycled through conventional melts.

Recyclability: 5% (Industrial Pyrolysis/Roads)
Everyday Examples:
Crisp & Chips Sachets Biscuit Wrappers Toothpaste Tubes

Action: Collect in eco-bricks or designated dry waste centers for plastic-modified bitumen road paving.
